Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Calorie Tracker mobile application handles your data. Your privacy is important to us, and we are committed to being transparent about our data practices.

1. Information Collection

Calorie Tracker is designed as a local-first application. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal identification information (PII) to our servers. Any data you enter (such as height, weight, or food logs) remains strictly on your own device.

2. Use of Data

The data entered into the app is used exclusively for the purpose of calculating nutritional targets and displaying your progress within the application.

3. Data Storage and Security

All application data is stored in the app's private storage on your Android device. We do not maintain an online database of your personal metrics. Deleting the application will remove all data stored within it.

4. Third-Party Services

We do not share your data with third parties. The app does not contain advertisements, analytics trackers, or social media plugins that monitor your activity.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. Since no data is transmitted to us, we do not have access to any such information.
